Welcome aboard.

You're using a living system, mid-build — and you're helping shape it.

What we're building

FoveAI is a persistent AI companion — one mind, many surfaces. Jenna remembers you across sessions, speaks with a real voice, inhabits a 3D avatar, follows you into your IDE as a coding partner, and (soon) into XR. Underneath sits our own memory architecture — foveated memory — which keeps what's relevant sharp and compresses the rest, the way your own attention does.

The goal is an assistant that feels less like a stateless chatbot and more like a colleague who was already in the room.

What you get as a tester

  • Full access — chat, voice, the IDE companion, XR surfaces. No feature is held back.
  • Your own memory workspace — your conversations are yours, scoped to your account.
  • Cloud models on the house — a metered daily allowance of hosted NVIDIA models (nvidia-free) alongside the local ones. Want more? Paste your own free build.nvidia.com key in settings → Bring your own model — unlimited, on NVIDIA's dime, still through our memory pipeline.
  • A running gift ledger — your Beta Charter on the ignition screen accrues the value of your access, month by month. Early testers keep the best standing.
  • A direct line — the feedback channel on your settings page goes to the developer, not a ticket queue.

What we'd love from you

  • Use it for real things. Chat, let her help you code in your IDE, try voice. Real usage exposes what demos never will.
  • Report the weird. She repeats herself? Forgets something she shouldn't? The IDE agent fumbles an edit? That's signal — send it through the feedback form.
  • Request features. Missing something you expected? Ask. The feedback channel takes feature requests too — and the admin can send you follow-up questions directly.
  • Criticize freely. Polite silence doesn't help. If something feels slow, confusing, or pointless — say so. Negative feedback is the most valuable kind here.

Honest expectations

  • This is a beta: things will break, restart, or behave oddly. That's the point of the exercise.
  • The local model is fast but small — she can be brilliant and occasionally very confident about nonsense. Check important output.
  • Your data stays on this machine. Nothing you say is sent to third parties unless you use a cloud model profile.

Ready? Head to the console or visit your settings page — the feedback form and IDE download are waiting there.
